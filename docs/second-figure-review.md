# Second figure review: make the visual argument work

Reviewed 9 September 2026. This pass responds to the reader's specific examples and rechecks the entire existing collection. It separates three kinds of evidence: mathematical tests, inspection of rendered compositions, and actual browser interaction. Passing a geometry test does not establish aesthetic quality.

Each new experiment is one semantic listening unit. Its selected chart, component, mode, time or orbital parameters supply the reader's current source. Hidden equations, controls and reference diagrams are not all queued again as separate explanations. Provider-mocked browser checks verify that changing the model changes the actual narrator request.

The wave uses the first-order transverse displacement discussed in [Carroll's GR notes, §6](https://preposterousuniverse.com/wp-content/uploads/grnotes-six.pdf). The embedding is also checked directly by differentiating the surface and numerically integrating its radial metric.

## Scientific drawing and mathematical type

Primary trajectories and reference constructions have separate line weights. Dashed lines identify tangents, comparisons and far-side geometry where appropriate; they are not an arbitrary decorative treatment. Process connections use perpendicular entries and exits with rounded bends. Physical displacement vectors and light rays remain straight when the mathematics requires them to be straight.

Native tables now use 15–16px prose and at least 18px base mathematical type. They scroll locally on small screens. The source matrix and static curvature matrix received larger typeset entries and explicit narrow-screen compositions. The calculation map also becomes a vertical sequence, instead of shrinking six boxes into an unreadable thumbnail.

The original five color families remain stable. A second level distinguishes Riemann, Ricci, scalar and Weyl curvature; metric volume; the connection; four-velocity; density and pressure; action; coframe; and torsion. The implementation identifies objects from notation and context, rather than coloring every occurrence of a letter. In particular, a generic early-chapter tensor is not automatically stress–energy, a squared chart coordinate is not four-velocity, and a radius or ordinary function called R is not automatically scalar curvature. Indices and operators stay neutral. Both palettes exceed 4.5:1 contrast against the book and figure backgrounds, and original mathematical token streams are checked against uncolored KaTeX.
